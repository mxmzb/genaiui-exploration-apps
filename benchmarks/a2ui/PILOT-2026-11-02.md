Implementation: CopilotKit 1.83.0, A2UI 1.0 candidate; the app's own runtime,
18-component catalog and React workspace. GPT-5.4 via OpenRouter Azure EU, low
reasoning, ZDR, one generation per trial, no retries. All eight variants are
available in the app at http://127.0.0.1:3003/.

**Total paid spend: $1.263735 of the authorized $30 ceiling.** Sixteen paid
calls, one for each transport × strategy × task. No model calls were made during
regrading or button-dispatch checks. The persistent ledger contains no outstanding
unknown-charge reservations.

## Live outcomes and grading review

Twelve of sixteen trials meet every automatic requirement, including exact
strategy compliance. All sixteen completed and passed the native operation
contract and structural action checks after applying the installed Markdown
parser's normal recovery. Thirteen meet the content rubric.

Three C02 comparisons (tool/batch, tool/fields, Markdown/components) omit the
neighborhood. The rubric requires it, while the task says “identity” and the
comparison-specific app instructions explicitly list title but not neighborhood.
Treat this as a task-specification ambiguity pending clarification, not a clean
reliability failure. Future runs should explicitly name title and neighborhood
for every framework, with a versioned fixture change.

The Markdown/fields C03 response has twelve missing closing braces in its
id/costs seed operations. CopilotKit's `extractFenceOperations` recovers them; the
final rendered content and action bindings are correct. Raw-format validity
therefore fails while the native contract passes. It also builds the template in
two setup operations instead of the requested single createSurface. This preserves
field-wise filling but fails our exact strategy check. Record this distinction;
do not claim that the displayed UI was broken or that an extra model retry fixed it.

The initial evaluator conflated raw JSON parsing with contract and action
validation. An offline regrade now uses the same public extraction/normalization
functions as the renderer and retains raw validity as an independent verdict.
Original artifacts remain intact. No paid calls were repeated.

“Run end” is when the agent run resolves. Actions stay disabled until completion
in every variant. Timeline states count observed changes to records, populated
metrics, pending values or enabled buttons; they are not animation smoothness scores.

*Provider completion tokens include reported reasoning tokens. Raw usage records
preserve reasoning and cache details separately. Cached inputs differ across
trials, so total cost is observed billing, not a controlled uncached price comparison.

The replay fixtures hold the final tree/data constant: records reveal complete
apartments sequentially, fields reveal pending values and fill them incrementally,
and component updates can reveal parts of many records together. Live compositions
also differ. Field-wise C03 output is substantially longer than record-wise output
in this pilot; repetition is needed to quantify that effect confidently.

The actual browser viewport was 1280×720, despite requesting 1280×900. It is recorded
in each artifact. Long prompts and benchmark controls put the first title/metric
pair below the initial viewport in these runs, so first-meaningful-on-screen values
are null. Do not substitute complete-card timings for visible user-perceived latency.
Fix/standardize the measurement viewport before a publication run. The browser
reported visibility as visible. Images and fonts remain remote; their decoding and
loading are not part of content correctness or complete-card timings.
